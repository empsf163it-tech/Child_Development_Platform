AGES & STAGES

A journey in little steps.

Children develop in different patterns. Use milestones as friendly signposts.

BIRTH

Early foundations

Bonding, listening, movement and early communication begin forming the foundation.

6 MONTHS

Curiosity grows

Babies increasingly interact with people and objects while exploring.

1 YEAR

Little explorer

Communication, imitation, movement and exploration become more active.

2 YEARS

Growing independence

Children often become more expressive, purposeful and eager to participate.

Connection first.

Warm responsive moments give children a secure base from which to explore.

Confidence through practice.

Small opportunities to move, choose and try again build confidence.
